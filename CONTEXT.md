# Burrow Brigade

Backyard artillery teams take turns positioning a captain and firing one volley.

## Language

**Charge**: A held shot whose power grows until the player releases it. It is discarded when play is interrupted.
_Avoid_: Power preset

**Shot power**: The strength of a Seed Pod or Hop Bomb launch chosen by the duration of its charge.

**Volley**: A fired attack and its resulting projectile, blast and settling. One volley uses the captain's turn.
