# Burrow Brigade

A friendly, ridiculous turf war between two crews of burrowing creatures.

## Language

**Crew**: Three creatures fighting together. _Avoid_: faction.
**Captain**: The creature currently allowed to move and fire. _Avoid_: selected pawn.
**Turn**: One captain's opportunity to move and use one weapon. _Avoid_: round.
**Volley**: A fired weapon and the ensuing explosion and settling. _Avoid_: attack animation.
**Turf**: Solid ground that can be removed by explosions. _Avoid_: backdrop.
**Sudden tide**: Rising water that ends stalemates. _Avoid_: overtime.
